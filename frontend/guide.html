PulseTrade Guide

Use this page as the plain-English map for the entire site.

1. Start With Data

Use the CSV dropdown to choose a replay file. The metadata box shows the file name, event count, start and end timestamps, and detected schema.

To add your own data, choose a CSV from your computer and press Upload. If the file has the required columns, it appears in the dropdown automatically.

2. Replay Historical Markets

Press Load CSV, then use Replay to play events through the C++ matching engine. Step advances one event at a time. Jump moves to a timestamp.

Replay speed controls how quickly historical events move through the dashboard.

3. Watch The Order Book

The Level II book shows bids and asks by price level. The best bid is green and the best ask is red. Bar width shows relative size at each level.

The depth chart converts each side into cumulative volume so you can see where liquidity is concentrated.

4. Read Trades And Charts

The trade tape streams executed trades with buy order ID, sell order ID, price, and quantity. New prints animate as they arrive.

The price chart tracks last traded price. The volume histogram shows traded volume over time.

5. Submit Manual Orders

Choose Buy or Sell, select Limit or Market, enter price and quantity, and submit. The order goes directly into the backend and the book updates live.

Market orders ignore the price field. Limit orders rest if they do not cross the opposite side.

6. Simulate Latency

Order latency delays strategy actions before they reach the engine. Market data latency delays what strategies see before deciding.

Watch pending events, missed opportunities, fill delay, and slippage to see how latency changes execution quality.

7. Compare Strategies

The strategy table compares market making, momentum, mean reversion, and order book imbalance models.

P&L, inventory, trades, win rate, drawdown, exposure, fees, volume, cancellations, modifications, and delay are tracked separately.

8. Run Optimization

Select a CSV, strategy, ranking metric, train window, and test window. Press Run Optimization to sweep parameter combinations.

The optimizer ranks configurations, shows the best setup, compares in-sample and out-of-sample results, and draws curves for the best run.

9. Inspect Systems Runtime

The Systems Runtime panel shows process phase, thread roles, queue capacity, dropped events, and synchronization guards.

Use it while simulation or replay is running to connect the trading workflow to processes, memory, concurrency, and backpressure.

10. Export Results

Press Export Results after optimization to download a CSV summary of parameter rankings, P&L, trades, drawdown, and volume.
